Club Activities

Anime Club is about relaxing and spending time together. Here is what you can expect during lunch.

Watch Anime

We choose an anime together and watch it during lunch. Share your suggestions with the group.

Chat and Relax

Talk about your favorite shows or just hang out. You do not need to seriously analyze the anime to enjoy the club.

Card Games

Card games are planned for future meetings. More details will be shared when they are ready.
